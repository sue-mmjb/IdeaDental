We straighten teeth with metal braces

Idea Dental does not offer Invisalign or clear aligners. We straighten teeth with traditional metal braces, a reliable, proven approach that works for children, teens and adults - including more complex cases that aligners can't handle.

If you came here looking for Invisalign, our team is happy to talk through what metal braces can do for your smile and plan treatment around your goals and budget.

Why metal braces

  • Proven and reliable for straightening teeth and correcting the bite.
  • Works for children, teens and adults, including complex cases.
  • Kids and teens can personalize them with colored bands at each adjustment.

The dentist reviews your bite at your consultation and fits a plan around your goals and budget.

Cost & payment

The cost of braces depends on how much movement your teeth need and your treatment plan, which the dentist confirms at your consultation.

Idea Dental is known for affordable pricing. Treatment finished in a single visit is paid in full that day; because braces are placed over multiple visits, the cost can be arranged on a payment plan, and we verify your insurance benefits before treatment.

Common questions

Do you offer Invisalign?

No. Idea Dental does not offer Invisalign or clear aligners. We straighten teeth with traditional metal braces, which are a reliable, proven option for children, teens and adults. Ask our team and we'll plan the right braces treatment for your smile.

How does Idea Dental straighten teeth?

With traditional metal braces - stainless steel brackets and archwires that gently move your teeth into position over a series of visits. They work for children, teens and adults, and the dentist plans treatment around your goals and budget.
