What Invisalign is

Invisalign® is a modern alternative to braces that lets you straighten your teeth without installing metal in your mouth. Instead, a series of custom plastic trays, called aligners, gradually move your teeth into place. Each aligner is custom-made to fit your mouth, so you don't have to worry about the mouth irritation that traditional braces can cause.

Because the aligners are removable, they're easy to keep clean, you can brush and floss normally, and since they're clear, most people won't even know you're wearing them unless you tell them.

Who Invisalign is for

  • Teens and adults who want a discreet, removable option.
  • Mild to moderate crowding, spacing or alignment issues.
  • People who don't want the look or food restrictions of metal braces.

Very complex cases may be better suited to braces. The dentist will tell you honestly which option fits your smile at your consultation.

Common questions about Invisalign

What is Invisalign and how does it work?

Invisalign is a way to straighten teeth using a series of clear, custom-made plastic aligners instead of metal braces. You wear each set for about one to two weeks, and they gradually move your teeth into place. The aligners are removable, so you can eat, brush and floss normally, and most people won't notice you're wearing them.

Is Invisalign better than braces?

Neither is universally better; it depends on your case. Invisalign is popular with teens and adults because it is nearly invisible and removable, while traditional or clear braces can handle more complex tooth movements. At your consultation the dentist can use the iTero scanner to show what Invisalign results could look like and recommend the best fit.

What is the iTero scanner?

The iTero scanner is a digital 3D scanner Idea Dental uses to create precise images of your teeth without the messy, uncomfortable putty impressions of the past. It makes appointments more comfortable and can also preview the results you might achieve with Invisalign before you begin treatment.
