What porcelain veneers are

Porcelain veneers are wafer-thin, custom-made shells of tooth-colored material designed to cover the front surface of teeth and improve their appearance. These shells are bonded to the front of the teeth, changing their color, shape, size or length.

We recommend veneers for anyone experiencing discoloration that can't be fixed with whitening, teeth that are damaged, or if you're simply unhappy with the shape or look of your natural teeth.

Who veneers are for

Veneers are a good option if you have discoloration whitening can't correct, chipped or damaged teeth, uneven or misshapen teeth, or gaps you'd like to close cosmetically. If your main goal is simply a brighter shade on healthy teeth, whitening may be a better fit — the dentist helps you choose.

What to expect

  1. Consultation. The dentist examines your teeth, discusses your goals, and confirms veneers are right for you.
  2. Preparation. The teeth are lightly prepared and an impression or digital scan is taken.
  3. Custom fabrication. Your veneers are made to match your desired color, shape and size.
  4. Bonding. The finished veneers are bonded to the front of your teeth.

Common questions about veneers

What are porcelain veneers?

Porcelain veneers are thin, custom-made shells bonded to the front of your teeth to improve their color, shape, size or length. They are a cosmetic option for teeth that are discolored, chipped or uneven, or for discoloration that whitening cannot fix, and they give a natural-looking, lasting result.

Are veneers or whitening right for me?

Whitening brightens the natural shade of healthy teeth, while veneers change the color, shape and size of teeth and can fix discoloration that whitening cannot. If your teeth are chipped, uneven or stained in a way whitening won't correct, veneers may be the better option. The dentist helps you decide at your consultation.
