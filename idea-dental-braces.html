What braces do

Even though there are many types of braces, traditional braces are still the most popular option. Great strides in technology have been made since they were first used, so they're becoming more comfortable and effective. Traditional braces use stainless steel brackets and archwires to move your teeth into their proper position, and many teens like to decorate them with different colored bands at every adjustment.

Common questions about braces

What types of braces do you offer?

Idea Dental offers traditional metal braces, clear (tooth-colored) braces, and clear aligners such as Invisalign, including options for teens and adults. Traditional braces remain the most reliable choice for complex cases, while clear braces and aligners are less noticeable. The orthodontic team helps you choose based on your goals and budget.

At what age should my child see an orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a child first see an orthodontist by around age seven. At that age treatment often isn't needed yet, but an early check lets the dentist monitor how the teeth and jaw are developing and catch problems while they are simpler to correct.

Is Invisalign better than braces?

Neither is universally better; it depends on your case. Invisalign is popular with teens and adults because it is nearly invisible and removable, while traditional or clear braces can handle more complex tooth movements. At your consultation the dentist can recommend the best fit for your smile.
